What Stride does

Stride is a personal brand assistant for students and early-career builders. It helps you turn real career moments into LinkedIn posts — without starting from a blank page.

You set your goals, tone, and content pillars. Stride helps identify post-worthy moments, drafts posts in your voice, and lets you review everything before publishing.

AI + human, not autopilot

Stride uses AI to help draft and organise ideas, but you stay in control. Nothing is published automatically.

Why we request access

Google account Used to sign you in and identify your Stride profile.
Google Calendar Read-only access to surface upcoming events as post ideas. Calendar data is fetched live and is never stored in our database.
LinkedIn Connects your LinkedIn profile and publishes posts only when you explicitly click "Publish to LinkedIn." Nothing is posted automatically.
Onboarding answers Your goals, tone, and content pillars are used to personalise drafts around your voice — not shared or used to train AI models.
