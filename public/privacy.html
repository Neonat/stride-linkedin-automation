Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What Stride is

Stride is a personal brand building tool that helps you turn your career moments into LinkedIn posts. It is currently in demo/beta and operated by an individual developer.

What data we collect

  • Google account info — your name, email address, and profile photo, collected when you sign in with Google.
  • Onboarding answers — your selected role, career aspirations, goals, tone, and content pillars.
  • Posts you create — drafts and published posts you create within Stride.
  • Career moments — events you manually log in the app.
  • Google Calendar events — read live from your calendar each time the dashboard loads. We do not store calendar event titles, times, or any calendar data in our database.

How we use your data

  • To generate a personal brand profile and LinkedIn post drafts in your voice.
  • To show you upcoming calendar events as post inspiration.
  • To track your posting streak and brand pillar coverage.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your profile and posts are stored in Supabase, a secure cloud database. Data is stored in the US. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth and Google OAuth.

Google Calendar access

Stride requests read-only access to your Google Calendar (calendar.readonly scope). We fetch your upcoming events live each session to suggest post-worthy moments. No calendar data is saved to our servers.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Google OAuth — sign-in and calendar access (privacy policy)
  • Agnes AI — used to generate post drafts
  • Vercel — hosting (privacy policy)

Data retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You can request deletion by emailing us. We will delete your profile, posts, and moments within 14 days of a request.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. To do so, contact us at the email below.
